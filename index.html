Experience

Formerly The Circle: Social Search

A local guide in every guest's hands.

Tuka began as a consumer app for finding information without the noise of ads and marketing. It has since shifted to hotels, pairing smart software with a simple front-desk device so every guest gets curated, locally vetted recommendations, and the hotel sees what guests search for, visit and love.

Visit tuka.world
1 Product one

Tuka for Hotels

A device at the front desk gives every guest an instant, app-free way into the hotel's digital concierge. One tap on the phone opens a local guide for the hotel's neighborhood.

  • Tap the device, ask in plain words, receive local recommendations
  • No app to download, with instant access
  • Hotel teams see what guests search for, how they get there and what performs
2 Product two

The guest app, with Thea

The second product is the guest-facing experience itself. Guests open the hotel's page, ask Thea, the concierge, what they feel like doing, and get answers that start with what is closest to their room.

  • "Discover Chicago the way locals do": search, or tap a category
  • Recommendations with walking times, booking and directions
  • A map of local picks and a saved trip

Design research

A community for blind and low-vision software professionals

In previous research, blind and low-vision software professionals (BLVSPs) expressed distress at the lack of a centralized community for sharing and discovering DIY tools, tips and tricks for more accessible software development. My team and I surveyed them and designed this platform around their considerations.

BLVSPs wireframe · interactive Figma prototype

Users indicated a desire for community, hence our high-fidelity design of a chat interface for each DIY tool posted.

We are currently in the prototyping cycle and iterating based on user feedback. Come back later for more pages.
